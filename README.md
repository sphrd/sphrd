### ⚡ The short version

I'm an electronics and software engineer. Analyzer eye and creative eye at once: one measures, debugs and optimizes, the other writes, designs and imagines.

- 🔬 **Silicon:** post-silicon validation, mixed-signal IC test and device characterization. Low-power VLSI, FPGA/VHDL, embedded firmware (AVR/ARM, SPI, I²C).
- 💻 **Software:** C++, Python, Java, TypeScript. Swift/SwiftUI on iOS. PyTorch and time-series models.
- 📄 **Research:** first author, [*Ultra-Efficient Nonvolatile Approximate Full-Adder With Spin-Hall-Assisted MTJ Cells for In-Memory Computing*](https://doi.org/10.1109/TMAG.2021.3064224), IEEE Transactions on Magnetics (2021). **5.2× lower power-delay product** than prior nonvolatile approximate adders.
- 🎓 **Studied:** M.Sc. Electronics Engineering at Politecnico di Milano · M.Sc. Digital Electronics at Shahid Beheshti University (top of class).
