### ⚡ Nutshell

I'm an electronics and software engineer. 
Analyzer eye and creative eye at once: 
one measures, debugs and optimizes, while the other writes, designs and imagines.

- 🏭 **Now:** ATE Application Engineer at Nash Technologies, Milan. Production test programs (C++/Java) for STMicroelectronics automotive and consumer ICs on Advantest V93000/SMT8 and T2000.
- 🔬 **Electronics:** post-silicon validation, mixed-signal IC test and device characterization. Low-power VLSI, FPGA (VHDL/Verilog), SPICE, embedded firmware (AVR/ARM, Arduino, SPI, I2C).
- 💻 **Software:** C++, Java, Python, MATLAB, Bash, VBScript, HTML/CSS.
- 📄 **Research:** first author, [*Ultra-Efficient Nonvolatile Approximate Full-Adder With Spin-Hall-Assisted MTJ Cells for In-Memory Computing*](https://doi.org/10.1109/TMAG.2021.3064224), IEEE Transactions on Magnetics (2021). **5.2× lower power-delay product** than prior nonvolatile approximate adders.
- 🎓 **Studies:** M.Sc. Electronics Engineering at Politecnico di Milano · M.Sc. Digital Electronics at Shahid Beheshti University (top of class).
